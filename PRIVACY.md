# Catabook privacy policy

*Last updated: 11 November 2026*

Catabook is a home-library app published by Epiwssa. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and how to delete it.

## What the app collects

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Email address and password | To sign you in and keep your library in sync across phones | Stored by Supabase, our database host. Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. |
| Your library: book titles, authors, ISBNs, covers, shelf locations, read status, notes and collections | This is the app's purpose | Stored in our Supabase database, visible only to you and to people you invite to your library. |
| Your display name, and the star ratings and reviews you write | So you and the people in your shared libraries can see what each other thought of a book | Stored in our Supabase database. Your name is visible only to people who share a library with you; reviews only to members of that book's library. |
| Library membership | So people you share an invite code with can see and edit the same library | Stored in our Supabase database. |

## What the app does not collect

- **Camera images.** The camera is used only to read barcodes. The images are processed on your phone and are never saved or sent anywhere.
- No location, contacts, advertising ID, or analytics.
- No ads. Your data is never sold or shared for advertising.

## Third-party book lookups

When you scan a barcode or search for a book to add, the app sends **only the ISBN or the search words you typed** to these public book databases, to fetch the title, author and cover:

- [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org) (Internet Archive)
- [Google Books](https://books.google.com)

No account details or library data are sent to them.

## Security

All connections between the app, Supabase and the book databases use encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Database rules ensure each library can be read only by its members.

## Storage and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account.

**To delete your account in the app:** open **Settings → Delete my account** and confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes:

- your account (email and password), your display name, and every rating and review you wrote;
- every library where you are the only member, with all its books and collections.

Libraries you share with other people stay for the remaining members, but you are removed from them.

**To ask for deletion without the app:** email **mrepiwssa@gmail.com** from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Catabook account". We delete the account and data as above within 30 days.

## Children

Catabook is not directed at children under 13.
